# BMO Ultra Short-Term Bond ETF (ZST.TO)

Type: Fund (ETF, very short-term bonds) | Focus: Canadian bonds that mature or reset within about one year, a "cash alternative" | Country: Canada | Trades on: Toronto Stock Exchange (ZST, CAD)
Also known as: ZST, BMO Ultra Short-Term Bond
As of: BMO product page and fact sheet (fees from the June 30, 2025 fact sheet)

## What it is
ZST holds bonds that mature (get repaid) in less than one year, or whose interest rates reset within one year. BMO lists it among its "cash alternative" ETFs. It is meant as a place to park money you may need soon while earning some interest, with very small price changes.

## Key facts
- Management fee: 0.15%. MER (total yearly cost): 0.17% (June 30, 2025 fact sheet).
- Duration: about 0.42 years (BMO product page; check the current fact sheet).
- Yield to maturity: about 2.90% (BMO product page).
- There is also an "accumulating" version, ZST.L, that reinvests interest instead of paying it out.

## Why it moves so little (duration)
Duration estimates how much a bond fund's price changes when interest rates move. With a duration under half a year, a 1 percentage point rise in rates would lower ZST's price by only about 0.4%. Compare that with a broad bond fund like XBB (about 6.7%) or a long-term fund like TLT (about 15%).

## Main risks (in plain words)
1. **Low return.** It earns roughly short-term interest rates, which may barely keep up with inflation.
2. **Interest rates fall.** Its yield drops quickly when the Bank of Canada cuts rates.
3. **Small credit risk** from the issuers of the bonds it holds.
4. **Not a bank deposit.** Unlike a savings account at a bank, it is not covered by deposit insurance, and its price can move slightly.

## What this means for a beginner
ZST is one of the steadiest things you can own in a brokerage account. It is useful for an emergency fund, money you'll need in the next year or two, or the "safe" part of a cautious portfolio. Its job is stability, not growth: over the long run, stocks are expected to earn much more.
